# Story brief

Create an interactive story about [protagonist] arriving at a lighthouse during [unusual event]. Keep the tone [desired mood]. The central question is [mystery or emotional conflict].

# Narrative structure

- Begin with a compelling scene that establishes place, stakes, and the protagonist's immediate goal.
- Offer meaningful choices that change what the reader learns or how relationships develop.
- Track only a small number of readable story flags.
- Provide at least three distinct endings that follow from earlier choices.
- Keep the story concise enough to complete in one sitting and make every branch feel intentional.

# Reader experience

Present one scene at a time with comfortable reading width, strong type contrast, and clear choice buttons. Show a subtle location or chapter label. Let readers revisit the story and restart without losing control of their current session.

# Acceptance criteria

- Every choice leads to a valid scene and every ending is reachable.
- The story remains understandable when a reader reaches any branch.
- Keyboard and touch navigation work, and progress feedback does not spoil future scenes.
