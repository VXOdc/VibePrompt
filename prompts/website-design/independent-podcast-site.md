# Objective

Build a responsive website for [podcast name], a show about [subject] for [audience]. The main action is listening to the latest episode.

# Page content

- A direct description of the show, its host, and its point of view
- A prominent latest episode with working playback or a clearly labeled listening link
- An episode archive with search or topic filters
- Episode pages with summaries, guests, transcripts when available, and platform links supplied by the publisher
- A simple subscription or contact path

# Visual direction

Use the supplied cover art and show identity as the starting point. Make episode titles and player controls easy to scan. Keep the page calm enough for long-form reading and make the newest episode visually obvious.

# Quality requirements

- Ensure playback controls have accessible labels and keyboard support.
- Include transcript links and descriptive text for audio where supplied.
- Make external listening platforms explicit and test every destination.
- Include page titles and social sharing metadata for episode pages.

# Acceptance criteria

- A new listener can identify the subject and begin listening quickly.
- Older episodes are easy to find and open.
- The experience is usable on mobile and desktop.
