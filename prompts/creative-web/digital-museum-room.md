# Exhibition concept

Create a digital exhibition around [collection or theme]. Its guiding idea is [curatorial question], and its audience is [visitors]. Keep the collection compact enough to explore in one visit.

# Visitor experience

- Open with a concise introduction that explains why these objects belong together.
- Present objects in a considered sequence with title, creator or origin when known, date when known, image, and readable interpretation.
- Connect objects through optional notes or a simple exhibition path.
- Provide a list view as an alternative to the visual gallery.
- Give visitors a clear way to return to the exhibition overview.

# Art direction

Let the subject determine the visual identity. Use generous reading space, consistent image treatment, and careful hierarchy. Keep all text available without hover and provide descriptive alternatives for images.

# Acceptance criteria

- Visitors understand the exhibition's premise and can navigate the complete collection.
- The sequence works on mobile, desktop, keyboard, and screen reader.
- Missing historical details are marked as unknown rather than fabricated.
