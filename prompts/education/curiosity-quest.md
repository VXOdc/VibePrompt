# Objective

Create a short learning game that helps [audience] understand [subject] through active exploration rather than a long lesson.

# Learning path

- Start with a concrete question or small challenge.
- Introduce one concept at a time through a visual experiment or choice.
- Respond to correct and incorrect answers with specific, encouraging explanations.
- Include a practice round and a concise summary of what the player explored.
- Let players retry without a penalty or forced account creation.

# Design and content

Use examples that are accurate, age-appropriate, and connected to the learning objective. Keep language plain and controls predictable. Use a visual metaphor that helps explain the subject rather than merely decorating the screen. Make the layout accessible to keyboard and touch users.

# Acceptance criteria

- Each activity teaches one identifiable idea.
- Feedback explains why an answer works.
- A player can complete or restart the experience independently.
- Progress is not represented as a judgment of the learner.
