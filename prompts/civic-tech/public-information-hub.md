# Objective

Create a trustworthy public information website for [office, service, or community organization]. Its audience is [community], and the priority information is [services, deadlines, locations, or contacts].

# Experience

Organize content around the questions a visitor needs answered. Provide clear labels, plain-language summaries, strong search or topic navigation, contact details, office hours, and paths to official source pages. Keep urgent notices visible without making routine information feel alarming.

# Design and accessibility

Use a calm, high-contrast palette and consistent typography. Make the page work at phone, tablet, and desktop widths. Support keyboard navigation, screen readers, zoom, and reduced motion. Use descriptive links and clear focus states. Do not fabricate policies, endorsements, statistics, or official status; represent unknown facts as editable content fields for the site owner.

# Privacy and trust

Do not collect sensitive personal information unless the product brief explicitly requires a secure, approved workflow. Explain what any form collects and where it goes. Keep contact paths and external links clearly labeled.

# Acceptance criteria

- A visitor can locate a service, its requirements, and its official contact path quickly.
- Every link and interactive control has a clear purpose and works.
- Content remains legible and usable with assistive technology and on small screens.
