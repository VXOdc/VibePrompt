# Brief

Create a distinctive identity for [cafe name or concept], a [kind of cafe] for [audience] in [place or setting]. The feeling should be [three useful adjectives].

# Deliverables

- A concise positioning statement and a short, specific tagline
- A name story and a clear tone of voice
- A compact color palette with accessible pairings
- Typography direction and a simple mark or wordmark concept
- Example menu language and a short launch announcement
- Applications for a storefront sign, menu, takeaway cup, and social post

# Creative rules

Anchor each visual and verbal choice in the cafe's audience and idea. Avoid generic coffee puns, stock imagery, invented awards, and claims about sourcing or history that were not provided. Show how the identity remains legible in small and large formats.

# Acceptance criteria

- Every deliverable feels like one coherent brand.
- The identity is specific enough to distinguish this cafe from a template.
- Copy is concise, usable, and grounded in the supplied concept.
